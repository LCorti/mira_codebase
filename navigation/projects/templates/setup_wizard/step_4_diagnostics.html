Should-Know (SK) and Really-Know (RK) methods help you understand different aspects of model behavior. SK methods test what the model should know to perform a task correctly, based on expected behavior or task requirements. RK methods reveal what the model actually relies on or attends to when producing outputs. Using both together helps you compare expected and observed behavior, providing a comprehensive view of model capabilities and limitations.
